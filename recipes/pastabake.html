Pasta Bake

Description

This is a delicious pasta bake recipe that combines al dente pasta with a savory sauce and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta (penne, rigatoni, or rotini)
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, combine cooked pasta, marinara sauce, ricotta cheese, and half of the mozzarella cheese. Mix well.
  4. Transfer the pasta mixture to a greased 9x13 inch baking dish. Sprinkle remaining mozzarella cheese and Parmesan cheese on top.
  5. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 10-15 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  6. Let pasta bake cool for 5 minutes before serving.
