Lasagna

Description

This chicken lasagna is made with tender chicken breasts, red sauce, and lots of mozzarella cheese for a fantastic flavor combination. Everyone who tastes it raves!

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, cook ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the skillet with the ground beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese, 1 cup of mozzarella cheese, and Parmesan cheese.
  6. Spread a thin layer of the meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Layer with noodles, ricotta mixture, and more meat sauce. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a layer of meat sauce on top.
  7. Sprinkle remaining mozzarella cheese on top.
  8. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  9. Let lasagna cool for 10 minutes before serving.
