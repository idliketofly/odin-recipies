Shrimp Pesto

Description

This shrimp pesto is a quick and easy dish that combines succulent shrimp with a vibrant pesto sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 cup pesto
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute.
  3. Add shrimp and cook for 2-3 minutes until pink and cooked through.
  4. Stir in pesto and cook for another minute until heated through.
  5. Sprinkle grated Parmesan cheese on top and serve immediately.
